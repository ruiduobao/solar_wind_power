## 15. [Country Dimension] Country Resource Competitiveness Matrix (Country Resource Matrix - Normalized)

![3_Country_Resource_Matrix](Z:\Mywork\论文\全球绿色能源生态评估_2025.12.24\制图\3.3风力光伏发电潜力差异对比\3_Country_Resource_Matrix.png)

### Method
*   **Objective**: Assess each country's resource advantage relative to the global average.
*   **Data processing**: Uses the **Relative Quality Index (RQI)**, where $RQI = Site\ CF / Global\ Mean\ CF$.
    *   **X axis**: Solar RQI. Values > 1 indicate above-global-average resources.
    *   **Y axis**: Wind RQI. Values > 1 indicate above-global-average resources.
*   **Color**: Grid emission factor (Grid EF).
*   **Interpretation**:
    *   **Above the diagonal**: The country's wind resources are more globally competitive than its solar resources.
    *   **Below the diagonal**: The country's solar resources are more globally competitive than its wind resources.
